# Human-Centered AI and the Challenge of Planned Obsolescence
## TPAC 2025 Information Architecture CG

### Description

As artificial intelligence becomes integral to daily life, the dynamic tensions between human-centered design and planned obsolescence grows critical. While planned obsolescence—deliberately designing products with limited lifespans to drive consumption—has plagued the technology industry, AI offers opportunities to create more sustainable solutions.

Human-centered AI prioritizes user needs, transparency, and long-term value over short-term profits. It advocates for systems that empower users, remain functional over time, and respect human agency—contrasting sharply with AI designed for built-in obsolescence through algorithmic degradation or forced migrations.

Information architecture (IA) is essential to this approach, determining how AI systems organize and present data. Thoughtful architecture creates flexible frameworks that adapt with user needs rather than forcing rigid structures that become obsolete. Current advances in intent taxonomies enable AI systems to understand and categorize user goals more effectively, creating semantic frameworks that evolve with changing user behaviors while maintaining consistency across interactions.

Equally crucial are emerging interoperability standards that prevent vendor lock-in and ensure seamless data portability between AI platforms. Plugin architectures, cross-platform compatibility frameworks, and standardized intent recognition protocols create ecosystems where users control their data and workflows independent of specific AI providers. By prioritizing accessibility and long-term usability, information architects ensure AI interfaces remain valuable as technology evolves.

Our keynote speaker and lightning talks can help us usher in an era of sustainable, user-empowering technology that grows more valuable over time.
