# Information Architecture Conferences

Most of these events have proceedings or online recordings. Some are no longer operating, but links are provided to archived content, where available 

## Active Events

### World Information Architecture Day
https://www.worldiaday.org
Annual IA celebration held by the World Information Architecture Association.

### IAC: The Information Architecture Conference (2019–present)
https://theiaconference.com
Annual information architecture conference held jointly by the IA Institute and Kunverj (2018) and then by Kunverj from 2019 to the present after the IA Institute closed its operations.

### Reframe IA: The Information Architecture Round Table (2013–present)
https://www.iaroundtable.org
Bridging academia & industry, and research, theory & practice, the Information Architecture Roundtable is a series of annual events to discuss the transformation of information architecture beyond its original context of web sites. 
Roundtable discussions push us towards thinking of information architecture as a discipline and profession.
